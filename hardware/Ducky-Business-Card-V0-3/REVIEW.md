# Ducky Card V0.3 hardware review

**Prototype review, November 22, 2026. Hold production assembly.** The PCB is routed. Firmware support, the coin-cell contact and the vendor findings below need attention before ordering a batch.

## Circuit and power

| Circuit | Finding and acceptance check |
|---|---|
| Firmware compatibility | The production-pond image on main still lists PC1/PC2/PC3 as unused. PC1 stays at its reset input state, so firmware supplies no intentional power to the mic or the tag’s I²C side. Build a V0.3-specific image that enables PC1 before listening or I²C, keeps it on through four-blow setup and verified EEPROM completion, and sequences pull-ups, power-off and wake for the actual KC tag. Passive RF reading uses the phone field. |
| Switched rail | R1 and 100 nF give a nominal 47 µs time constant. At 3.2 V the ideal upper bound on initial resistor current is 6.8 mA. Nominal initial slope is 0.068 V/µs. The mic draws about 120 µA typical and the KC tag about 110 µA typical during a 3.3 V EEPROM write under ST’s test conditions. Combined, the estimate is 0.23 mA and a 0.11 V resistor drop. Scope U2 pin 8 against local ground on fresh and weak cells. Verify a continuous rise below 1 V/µs and at least 1.8 V throughout the write, including when a phone is present. |
| LEDs and battery | The 24 anodes connect directly to VCC. Nine CJ3400s switch groups of two or four cathodes. R1 limits peripheral-rail current only. There are no LED series resistors or external gate pull-downs. Startup gates depend on MCU initialization. Current sharing depends on LED forward voltage and source impedance. With the intended CR2032, capture MCU VDD and bank current during reset, the brightest scene and the 12 s hold. The existing 10 MHz image needs at least 2.7 V to stay in its specified speed range. |
| Sleep | A prior physical card drew 25.5 mA during one four-LED hold and 3.0–3.7 mA after great/little luck with the LEDs dark. The meter’s 0.0 mA reading for the other fortunes only establishes a reading below about 1 mA. The proposed PIT re-entry fix compiled in the shared session and lives on a separate branch. Repeat through URL expiry and final sleep on identified firmware, disconnect UPDI, and measure in µA. V0.3 runtime and the <1 µA design target have no bench result. |
| NFC and microphone routing | The KC AC0/AC1 terminals route to J1, PB0/PB1 carry SCL/SDA, and PC0 samples the mic output on ADC1. The bus relies on MCU pull-ups. SCL and SDA have about 64 and 63 mm of routed track. The mic trace is about 66 mm. Check bus rise times and EEPROM readback, and compare the ADC noise floor with the LED banks on and off. U3’s 0.40 mm acoustic opening is the single non-plated hole. Measure NFC tuning/read distance with cell and holder installed. |
| Touch and programming | SW1 uses 7.8 mm sensing copper within an approximately 12 mm keepout. The attached board has a left-entry trace, about 28 mm to PC3, and rear copper is clear within a measured 5.9 mm radius. Qualify front taps, rear grips and nearby RF. The cloud simulation used a test board. UPDI is TP1, ground is TP3, and V0.3 has no dedicated TP2 VCC pad. |
| Battery contact and stencil | BT1 has a 14 mm exposed negative contact. The bottom paste Gerber flashes the entire 14 mm disc as well as the holder tabs. J1 also has paste apertures at two printed antenna terminals. Confirm the desired contact finish and stencil openings before assembly. Seven GND via centers lie 8.30–9.06 mm from the cell center. The Gerbers show copper under the rim and no exposed copper in the checked 7.05–10 mm annulus. Inspect mask wear, clearance and cell seating on a sample. |

Electrical limits and wake behavior depend on the purchased device and its errata. [ST KC datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/st25dv04kc.pdf) · [ST KC errata](https://www.st.com/resource/en/errata_sheet/es0617-st25dv04kc-st25dv16kc-and-st25dv64kc-device-limitations-stmicroelectronics.pdf) · [ATtiny1616 datasheet](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/MCU08/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/ATtiny1614-16-17-DataSheet-DS40002204A.pdf) · [V0.3-incompatible pin list](../../firmware/production-pond/src/pins.h) · [GPIO configuration](../../firmware/production-pond/src/unused_pins.h).

## Verification

- KiCad 10.0.8 DRC after zone refill, including schematic parity and all severities, went from nine antenna errors to **zero violations, zero unconnected items and zero parity issues**. No DRC exclusions were added. ERC went from **five errors and 105 warnings** to **zero errors and 105 warnings**. Of those, 70 concern unspecified imported pin types, 34 flag differences from installed library symbols, and one flags the one-pin LED10/PB4 stub. Imported pin typing limits ERC’s ability to detect electrical mistakes. [DRC report](checks/drc.rpt) · [ERC report](checks/erc.rpt).
- The board outline measures 88.9 × 50.8 mm along the routed centerline. Stackup is two copper layers, 35 µm each, on a 1.6 mm board. There are 423 routed segments and 92 board vias at 0.60/0.30 mm plus two plated antenna holes at the same size. Minimum routed track width is 0.20 mm. A supplemental KiCad run with 0.10 mm minimum copper clearance, 0.15 mm silk clearance and stroke, and 1.0 mm text height also returned zero. Imported graphic artwork needs separate fabrication-layer inspection. [JLCPCB capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities/).
- J1’s KiCad copper polygons have zero area difference on both faces at 0.1 µm polygonization tolerance. Independently overlaying the full original and corrected Gerbers found zero back-copper difference and under 0.00001 mm² on the front at the crossover. The mask, stencil, silkscreen, outline and drill drawing commands matched after removing metadata.
- The Gerber ZIP contains two copper, two mask, two silk and two paste layers, board outline, two drill files and the job descriptor. BOM/CPL locations were generated with the project’s Fabrication Toolkit rotation corrections. Check pin 1, LED polarity and the holder orientation in the assembly preview before purchase.

## JLCPCB manufacturing checks

The same Gerber ZIP used for the vendor check is in [production](production/). Its SHA-256 is `0654b85c2a0537a52a33f06d5fb688d123e679edb61bd7ae5a0f4aeb82c41402`. The portal matched all ten BOM codes and displayed all 40 bottom-side placements. The [populated underside](checks/assembly.jpg) shows the KiCad model.

| Check | JLCDFM result | What needs attention |
|---|---|---|
| Bare PCB | 32 Danger, 362 Warning, 275 Good | 28 silkscreen-to-hole and two silkscreen-to-pad entries. Two more Danger entries report 0.09 mm pad-to-copper clearance on the bottom. The viewer leaves the check name blank. The reported rectangle size matches an R1 land, although the designator was not confirmed. Review the affected artwork and copper with fabrication engineering. |
| SMT assembly | 63 Danger, 0 Warning, 34 Good | 27 “pin without pad” entries, one per lead on all nine MOSFETs. Eleven inner-edge flags cover U2 and U3. Twelve left-edge and eleven right-edge flags cover U1 and U3, including values displayed as 0 mm. U1 and U2 each have one 0.17 lead-contact-ratio entry. Require a pin-1 and lead-overlap check against the actual assembly model before ordering. |
| Drill interpretation | Two drill files | The viewer labeled both inputs as plated. The submitted NPTH file explicitly declares the microphone’s 0.40 mm acoustic hole non-plated. Confirm it stays non-plated in the CAM drill map. |

The final CPL was re-uploaded and the assembly analysis rerun. The 27 MOSFET alerts persisted after the rotation update. An independent overlay compared the supplier’s numbered pad centres with each physical KiCad land as seen from the underside. For all 40 parts, the submitted angle gives the smallest error among rotations in 45° increments. The largest pin-centre difference is 0.15 mm for the MOSFETs and 0.043 mm for the MCU. BT1 has two electrically interchangeable orientations 180° apart. Verify its insertion direction mechanically. This check establishes pin order and angle against the catalog footprint. The vendor assembly model and stencil still need sign-off.
